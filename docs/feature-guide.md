# Folio — Feature Guide

A quick reference for everything Folio can do. Share this alongside a login so new people know what they're looking at.

## Planner

**What it is:** a simple, date-based task list — but a sparse one. Unlike a normal calendar, it doesn't show every day of the month; it only ever shows dates you've actually put something on. Add a task to any date, and only that date appears.

**How to use it:**
- Click **Planner** in the sidebar to open it
- Pick a date (the button at the top opens a calendar), type a task, hit enter
- Check tasks off as you finish them
- The top section, **"Up Next,"** always shows today's tasks first — or the nearest upcoming date if today is empty

**Where else you'll see it:** a scrolling ticker bar at the very top of the app shows today's (or tomorrow's — there's a toggle) undone tasks automatically, so you don't even need to open Planner to see what's coming up. You can pause the scroll with the button on the right.

**Bonus:** the AI chat can read your Planner too — try asking "what do I need to do today?"

## Notes & Folders

**What it is:** the core of Folio — write notes, organize them into folders (and subfolders).

**How to use it:** click **+ New Note**, choose a folder (or leave it in your general library), start typing. Drag any note in the sidebar onto a folder to move it there. Right-click a note for more options (pin, lock, color, delete).

---

## Tags

**What it is:** a lighter-weight way to group notes than folders — one note can have several tags at once, unlike folders where a note only lives in one place.

**How to use it:** open a note, click **Add tag**, type a word. Tags you've used appear in the sidebar under "Tags" — click one to instantly filter to just those notes.

---

## Search

**What it is:** a search bar in the sidebar that looks across *all* your notes at once, by title and by content — not just the folder you're currently browsing.

---

## AI Chat ("Ask your notes" / "Ask AI")

**What it is:** an AI assistant that answers questions using your own notes as its source — and can write directly into a note for you.

**How to use it:**
- Click **Ask your notes** in the sidebar for a general chat across your whole library
- Or open a note and click the floating **Ask AI** button (bottom-right) to ask about just that one note
- If it can't find an answer in your notes, it'll offer to "search generally instead" using its own broader knowledge
- Ask it to draft or summarize something, and you'll get an **"Insert into note"** button — click it to actually add that text to your note (nothing gets added without you clicking this)

---

## Note styles

**What it is:** give an individual note its own visual look, independent of your overall app theme — six options: Default, PDF Page, Notebook, Chalkboard, Kraft Card, and Terminal.

**How to use it:** open a note, click the paint-bucket icon in the toolbar, pick a style. Purely visual — doesn't change your content.

---

## App themes

**What it is:** change the color palette of the whole app — six built-in themes, or build your own with the Custom option (pick your own background, accent, and text colors).

**How to use it:** click the palette icon near the top of the sidebar.

---

## Locking a note

**What it is:** password-protect an individual note. A locked note's content is hidden everywhere — including previews and hover tooltips in the sidebar — until you unlock it with the password.

**How to use it:** open a note, click the lock icon in the toolbar, set a password.

---

## Markdown export

**What it is:** download any note as a real `.md` file — useful for backing up a note or moving it into another app.

**How to use it:** open a note, click the download icon in the toolbar.

---

## Accounts

**What it is:** you can log in either with a Replit account, or with a plain email and password — your choice, both work the same way once you're in.
